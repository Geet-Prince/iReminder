## What iReminder does *not* use

For clarity, iReminder contains no:

- Apple code, assets, frameworks, or documentation
- Advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs
- Social login, crash reporting, or telemetry services
- Third-party fonts, images, or media in the app itself

The only third-party visual asset is the Icons8 icon credited above. The
Reminders interface you see inside the app is served live by Apple from
`icloud.com`; it is not redistributed as part of iReminder.
